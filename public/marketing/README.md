# Marketing images

Put your own photos here, then point to them from `src/lib/marketing.ts`.

Examples:
- `/marketing/slide-1.jpg` — onboarding slide 1
- `/marketing/slide-2.jpg` — onboarding slide 2
- `/marketing/slide-3.jpg` — onboarding slide 3
- `/marketing/promo-fund.jpg` — home fund promo

Recommended size: **1200×900** (4:3) JPG or WebP, under 300 KB.

Until you add files, the app uses curated Unsplash URLs in `src/lib/marketing.ts`.
